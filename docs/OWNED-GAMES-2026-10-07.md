## How to use this record

- For a request about **my library**, use all visible entries here, not only the installed-game list. Keep the [historical installed inventory](INSTALLED-GAMES-MOD-GUIDE.md) separate.
- The screenshots are the original evidence; the text below is a searchable transcription. Trademark glyphs are omitted in the transcription without merging editions.
- An ellipsis marks a cropped label. Do not invent the missing edition or identify a cropped row as a different release with a similar name. Titles already established by local records may use their full name.
- Overlapping rows at screenshot boundaries are retained. Repeated cropped prefixes can refer to distinct entries; do not count them as verified duplicate licenses or collapse them automatically.
- Counters such as `13/14`, `74/77` and `34/35` appear in the screenshots. The omitted entries and reason for those counters are unknown. This is the user's current visible ownership list, not a complete machine-readable license export. Absence means **not shown**, not definitive non-ownership.
- The date is the snapshot date, not a purchase date. Steam's activity headings are recorded as displayed; month headings alone do not establish their year.
