# Bayonetta — Curated Recommendations

## First playthrough

**Core:** stock gameplay and a stable resolution/display mode. Add Proper PS4 Button Prompts only when it matches the controller, and No-Intro only after a clean baseline.

**Optional graphics:** one lightweight color preset, but not while diagnosing the neural/helper chain. Preserve the original art direction as an easy toggle.

**Avoid by default:** trainer cheats, model replacement, and broad combat/input automation before learning the original game.

## After familiarity

**Strong candidate for practice:** BayonettaTrainer V2's infinite-enemy-health, timer, turbo, stick-spin, or mouse-spin tools, with damage/kill/currency cheats left off unless explicitly wanted.

**Optional photo mode:** No-HUD-No-Effects in a separate ShaderToggler profile.

## Post-game

**Interesting:** model, costume, voice, music, and broader trainer/Lua modifications. No expansion-scale campaign is currently verified.

## Graphics combinations

1. **Simple:** dgVoodoo2 only where required for compatibility/resolution, no neural layer. Recommendation **Strong/Conditional**, difficulty **2**.
2. **Locally proven research stack:** dgVoodoo2 + x86 ReShade/Feeder + x64 RenoDX helper at 100%/4K. Recommendation **Experimental**, operation confidence **High**, moving-image-quality confidence **Medium–Low**, difficulty **5**.
3. **Performance upscaling/MFG:** no locally proven path. Feeder is full-resolution and incompatible with OptiScaler/Smooth Motion; research as a separate future profile. Recommendation **Untested**, confidence **Low**.
4. **ENB/ReShade preset on top of the neural stack:** recommendation **Avoid until isolated** because of proxy and color/effect-order conflicts.
